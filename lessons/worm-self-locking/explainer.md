## winch: The winch
[[scroll top]] Here is a small winch. <short pause> [[arrow part:motor "motor"]] A twelve volt motor [[arrow part:gearbox "worm gearbox"]] turns a worm gearbox, [[arrow part:drum "drum"]] which winds a rope onto a drum, [[arrow part:load "load"]] lifting a two kilogram load.
[[unmark]] At one point two seconds, the power switches off. <short pause> Before we watch, decide what you think the load will do. [[quiz predict-hold]]

## screw: A worm is a screw
[[scroll block:a-worm-is-a-screw]] [[spotlight gearbox]] [[pin part:gearbox/worm "the worm: a screw"]] First step. Look at the worm. <short pause> It's really just a screw.
[[unmark]] [[arrow part:gearbox/mesh "wheel"]] And the wheel it drives is like the nut.
[[unmark]] [[unpin]] [[pin part:gearbox/mesh "the wheel: the nut"]] [[highlight "trying to make the screw turn"]] With the power off, the load tries to turn the wheel backwards, so the wheel's teeth push along the thread. [[highlight off]] [[unpin]] [[spotlight off]] [[quiz worm-is-screw]]

## slope: The thread's slope
[[scroll block:the-thread-s-slope-the-lead-angle]] Second step: how steep is that thread?
[[box figure:lead-triangle "one turn, unrolled"]] Imagine unwrapping one turn and laying it flat. <short pause> You get a ramp. Its angle is called the lead angle.
[[unmark]] [[arrow figure:lead-triangle@280,270,120,40 "one start"]] With one thread, one start, it's a very gentle ramp. [[unmark]] Here's a quick one. [[quiz steeper-ramp]]

## working: Working out the angle
[[scroll block:working-out-the-lead-angle]] Now let's put a number on it. <short pause> The rise comes from the tooth size and the number of starts, and the length from the worm's diameter.
Follow the worked example, then try ours. [[quiz lead-angle]]

## friction: Friction's limit
[[scroll block:friction-s-limit-the-friction-angle]] Third step, and it's a separate idea: friction.
[[highlight "tilt the ramp up slowly"]] Put a block on a ramp and tilt it slowly. <short pause> At first it stays put. Then, at some angle, it slides.
[[highlight off]] That steepest angle is the friction angle. It depends only on how grippy the surfaces are. [[quiz friction-angle]]

## rule: The rule
[[scroll block:the-rule-compare-the-two-angles]] Now the two angles meet. <short pause> The wheel's tooth sits on the thread like a block on a ramp.
[[box figure:worm-ramp@0,0,380,360 "holds"]] If the thread is gentler than the friction angle, friction holds. [[unmark]] [[box figure:worm-ramp@380,0,380,360 "slides"]] If it's steeper, the load slides down the thread.
[[unmark]] [[highlight "Just shallow enough, so it holds"]] Ours is three point six degrees, against about four. <short pause> Just shallow enough. [[highlight off]] [[quiz what-unlocks]]

## price: The price
[[scroll block:the-price-efficiency]] But nothing is free. <sigh> The same friction that holds the load also fights the motor while it lifts.
[[highlight "only about 45 % of the motor's power reaches the drum"]] Less than half the motor's power reaches the drum. [[highlight off]] [[quiz worm-price]]

## starts: More starts
[[scroll block:more-starts-less-holding]] Let's test the rule. Give the worm four starts, <short pause> and the thread gets much steeper, about fourteen degrees. Make your prediction first. [[quiz predict-four]]
