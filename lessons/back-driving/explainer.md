## hook: Push a motor
[[scroll top]] Push a robot's leg by hand with the power off. <short pause> It resists more when the motor's leads are joined together. Why?

## emf: Spinning makes a voltage
[[scroll block:spinning-makes-a-voltage]] Remember the back-EMF: spin a motor and it makes a voltage, k times omega. <short pause> It's a generator, whether you want one or not. [[quiz emf]]

## brake: Close the circuit
[[scroll block:close-the-circuit-feel-the-brake]] [[highlight "it pushes against the motion"]] Join the terminals, and that voltage drives a current. And current in a motor makes torque, <short pause> pushing against the motion. [[highlight off]] [[quiz brake-steps]]

## watch: Open, resistor, short
[[scroll quiz:predict-short]] Which stops the wheel sooner: a resistor, or a direct short? [[quiz predict-short]]
[[scene brake]] [[scroll scene:brake]] [[play-until 0.1]] The wheel spins the motor, and current flows backwards through the resistor. [[play]] [[wait-scene]] Slower wheel, less voltage, less current, <short pause> gentler brake.

## tau: How fast
[[scroll block:how-fast-it-stops]] Because the brake is proportional to speed, the wheel slows exponentially. [[highlight "The winding's own resistance sets the hardest brake possible"]] And the winding's own resistance sets the hardest brake possible. [[highlight off]] [[quiz tau-stop]]

## energy: Where the energy goes
[[scroll block:where-the-energy-goes]] The wheel's energy becomes heat, <short pause> shared between the winding and the resistor, in proportion to their resistances. [[quiz energy-split]]
