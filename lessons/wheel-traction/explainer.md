## hook: Spinning wheels
[[scroll top]] Slam a rover's throttle open, and its wheels spin. <short pause> It launches barely faster than a gentle start. Let's see why.

## grip: Grip has a limit
[[scroll block:grip-has-a-limit]] [[highlight "at most the"]] A tyre pushes through friction, and friction has a limit: the grip, times how hard the tyre is pressed down. [[highlight off]] [[quiz fmax]]

## accel: The fastest launch
[[scroll block:the-fastest-a-wheel-can-accelerate]] So the rover can accelerate at most at grip times g. <short pause> And our gearmotor can make five times the torque that needs. [[quiz predict-spin]]
[[scene launch]] [[scroll scene:launch]] [[play-until 0.2]] Full throttle. The wheel spins: its rim runs ahead of the rover. [[play-until 0.45]] The rover catches up, and the tyre grips. <short pause> Then, the brake.
[[play]] [[wait-scene]] And now the wheel is slower than the ground under it. A skid. [[quiz read-slip]]

## control: Using the grip
[[scroll block:using-the-grip-without-wasting-it]] [[highlight "Spinning a wheel does not push the rover any harder"]] Spinning a wheel doesn't push the rover any harder. It just wears the tyre and wastes energy. [[highlight off]]
So robots limit the wheel's torque to what the grip can use. That's traction control. [[quiz torque-limit]]
