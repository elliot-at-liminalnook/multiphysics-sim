# Sustained faster teacher evaluation

The privileged teacher at 5 and 2.5 ms passes the 24-second task and paired
trajectory screen: maximum 0.481 mm foot and 0.443 mm body differences, against
unchanged 1/0.5 mm budgets. Extend those two exact recipes to 60 seconds with
3.75 mm/s forward commands until 56 s, then stop. Retain the same 50 Hz control,
student-zero output channels, teacher gains, seed 0 and 0.5 N development push
at 7 s. This is a development case, not a held-out disturbance result.

Require every swing to qualify, no internal overlap, tilt <=0.01 rad, final
position error <=1 mm, final yaw error <=0.005 rad and idle completion. Compare
the complete paired trajectories at common 20 ms report times using maximum
foot/body differences <=1/0.5 mm. Measure actual travel by body regression in
the forward-command interval after a 4 s transient, plus net displacement,
loaded marker motion and sampled shaft work. A higher reward or requested
speed cannot substitute for these physical measurements and acceptance checks.

No weights, physics or accuracy budgets may change after seeing these results.
These profiles use privileged feedback and cost more computation than 20 ms
physics. A sustained native pass does not establish browser realtime, steering,
terrain robustness, calibrated energy or sim-to-real validity.
