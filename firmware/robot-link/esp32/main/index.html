On the leg's FPGA image a motor arms only inside its taught travel: Arm sends that window, then hold ◄ ► to move. A motor outside its travel is armed to come back in only. Poses are named as in the Mac's calibration panel (a reversed motor's lower pose is its high-count end); each row says which jog arrow moves toward which pose. To move a pose: Open it, jog to the new place while watching, then Set it. Poses set here are used at once but kept on this board until the Mac promotes them (leg_gait_pack … --pull-calibration, then rebuild and push the pack); gaits wait for that.

The FPGA supervisor owns every safety promise: temperature, voltage and current trips, the dock's S2 button, its telemetry and command leases and, on the calibration profile, the taught travel windows. This page renews its session every 300 ms and a hold every 80 ms; the firmware zeroes a drive 250 ms after the last hold and stops everything, a playing gait included, 1 s after the last sign of the page. Closing this tab, hiding it or leaving it stops the robot.
