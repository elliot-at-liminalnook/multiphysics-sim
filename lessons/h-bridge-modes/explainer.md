## hook: Stopping at the edge
[[scroll top]] A rover sees the edge of a table, and must stop its wheels, now. <short pause> Its driver has no brake pads. Only four switches.

## switches: Four switches
[[scroll block:four-switches]] [[box figure:bridge-modes "three ways to stop"]] Two switches on each side of the motor. <short pause> Diagonals drive it forward or backward. [[unmark]]
[[highlight "Brake"]] Both low switches on: the motor is shorted. [[highlight "Coast"]] Everything off: the motor is disconnected. [[highlight off]] [[quiz which-brake]]

## compare: Brake and coast
[[scroll block:brake-and-coast-side-by-side]] Braking uses the motor as a generator into a short. Coasting gives it no path at all. [[quiz predict-stop]]
[[scene stop]] [[scroll scene:stop]] [[play-until 0.45]] Drive, then stop. [[play]] [[wait-scene]] The braked wheel slows fast. <short pause> The coasting wheel, hardly at all. [[quiz coast-why]]

## plug: Reverse
[[scroll block:reverse-the-hardest-stop]] [[highlight "supply then adds to the back-EMF"]] A third way: drive it backward. Then the supply adds to the back-EMF, and the current is bigger than a stall. [[highlight off]] [[quiz plug-current]]

## which: Which stop when
[[scroll block:which-stop-when]] So: coast to save energy, brake for a quick stop, <short pause> and reverse only with a current limit.
