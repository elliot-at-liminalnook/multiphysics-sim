# Component catalog

Every annotated part in the registry, grouped by palette section. Generated by `sim-system catalog > library/CATALOG.md`; open a part in the builder's library for its full notes (how it works, equations, trade-offs, limits, parameter help) and datasheet.

## Actuators

### Torque servo — `actuator.servo`

An ideal torque-controlled servo: follows a torque command within a bandwidth and limits.

- Ports: command (signal in Torque), current (signal out Current), shaft (rotational)
- Pairs with: `rotational.inertia`, `part.pid_angle`

### Air gap with armature — `bridge.air_gap`

An air gap whose length is set by a moving armature: the heart of a relay or solenoid.

- Ports: a (magnetic), armature (translational), b (magnetic)
- Pairs with: `bridge.coil`, `translational.mass`, `translational.spring`

### Brushed DC motor — `bridge.brushed_motor`

A permanent-magnet brushed DC motor: voltage in, torque out.

- Ports: case (rotational), n (electrical), p (electrical), shaft (rotational)
- Pairs with: `rotational.inertia`, `rotational.worm_gear`, `rotational.ideal_gear`, `bridge.lead_screw`, `rotational.ground`, `electrical.switched_voltage_source`, `electrical.voltage_source`, `electrical.ground`, `rotational.speed_sensor`
- Datasheet: no-load speed 1000 rad/s; no-load current 0 A; stall current 6 A; stall torque 0.072 N·m

### Motor behind one plug — `bridge.motor`

A DC motor behind a single motor plug (winding, shaft and case, with temperature-dependent resistance).

- Ports: plug (composite), plug.electrical (electrical), plug.rotational (rotational), plug.thermal (thermal)
- Pairs with: `bridge.dual_drive`

### Brushless DC motor (with ESC) — `part.bldc_motor`

A brushless motor and its ESC: electronic commutation, the same speed-torque line as a brushed motor, no brushes.

- Ports: case (rotational), heat (thermal), n (electrical), p (electrical), shaft (rotational)
- Pairs with: `rotational.inertia`, `part.propeller`, `rotational.lossy_gear`, `robot.battery`, `robot.h_bridge`, `rotational.ground`, `thermal.capacitance`, `thermal.conductance`
- Datasheet: no-load speed 1149.822 rad/s; no-load current 0.5 A; stall current 100 A; stall torque 1.0384 N·m

### Brushed DC motor (equations) — `part.brushed_motor_eq`

A permanent-magnet brushed DC motor, defined in a .part file.

- Ports: case (rotational), n (electrical), p (electrical), shaft (rotational)
- Pairs with: `rotational.inertia`, `rotational.worm_gear`, `rotational.ideal_gear`, `rotational.ground`, `electrical.switched_voltage_source`, `electrical.ground`
- Datasheet: no-load speed 1000 rad/s; no-load current 0 A; stall current 6 A; stall torque 0.072 N·m

### Coreless DC motor (thermal) — `part.coreless_motor`

A brushed motor with an ironless rotor: tiny inductance and inertia, torque that fades as it heats.

- Ports: case (rotational), heat (thermal), n (electrical), p (electrical), shaft (rotational)
- Pairs with: `rotational.inertia`, `rotational.worm_gear`, `rotational.lossy_gear`, `thermal.capacitance`, `thermal.conductance`, `electrical.switched_voltage_source`, `electrical.ground`, `rotational.ground`
- Datasheet: no-load speed 1000.0006 rad/s; no-load current 0 A; stall current 6.6666 A; stall torque 0.08 N·m

### Propeller — `part.propeller`

A spinning propeller: thrust and drag torque grow with the square of speed, thrust fades as the aircraft climbs.

- Ports: airframe (translational), shaft (rotational)
- Pairs with: `part.bldc_motor`, `rotational.inertia`, `translational.mass`, `translational.load_force`
- Datasheet: ratio (measured) 7013.7146 rad/m; forward efficiency 0.0102 ; backdrive efficiency 0 ; self-locking no 

### Pull solenoid — `part.solenoid`

A coil that pulls an iron plunger in: strong at short stroke, weak when open, energy-consistent force from the changing inductance.

- Ports: frame (translational), n (electrical), p (electrical), plunger (translational)
- Pairs with: `translational.mass`, `translational.spring`, `part.hard_stop_linear`, `electrical.switched_voltage_source`, `electrical.ground`, `translational.ground`

### Stepper motor with microstepping driver — `part.stepper_motor`

A stepper: the driver sets the rotor's magnetic target angle; the rotor follows like a stiff spring, or slips a step if overloaded.

- Ports: case (rotational), shaft (rotational), target (signal in Angle)
- Pairs with: `part.angle_setpoint`, `rotational.inertia`, `part.timing_belt`, `bridge.lead_screw`, `rotational.ground`, `sensor.encoder`, `part.hard_stop_rotary`

### Voice-coil actuator — `part.voice_coil`

A coil in a permanent-magnet gap: force proportional to current in both directions, over its whole stroke.

- Ports: coil (translational), magnet (translational), n (electrical), p (electrical)
- Pairs with: `translational.mass`, `translational.spring`, `part.hard_stop_linear`, `sensor.force`, `electrical.controlled_voltage_source`, `translational.ground`

### Effective bounded position servo — `robot.effective_servo`

A fast position servo stand-in: spring-damper toward a target, limited by a speed–torque envelope.

- Ports: housing (rotational), shaft (rotational), target (signal in Angle), torque (signal out Torque)
- Pairs with: `part.angle_setpoint`, `rotational.inertia`, `part.pendulum_gravity`

### Motor with gearbox and winding heat — `robot.motor_unit`

A complete gearmotor: winding, rotor, gearbox with efficiency, backlash and compliance, and winding heat.

- Ports: current (signal out Current), n (electrical), p (electrical), shaft (rotational), speed (signal out AngularVelocity), torque (signal out Torque), winding (thermal)
- Pairs with: `robot.h_bridge`, `robot.battery`, `thermal.capacitance`, `rotational.inertia`, `part.pendulum_gravity`, `sensor.encoder`
- Datasheet: no-load speed 6.1856 rad/s; no-load current 0 A; stall current 3.2435 A; stall torque 6.2925 N·m

## Mechanical

### Wheel with rolling contact on the plane — `contact.wheel`

- Pairs with: `joint.revolute`, `bridge.brushed_motor`

Welds two planar bodies together.

- Ports: a (planar_frame), b (planar_frame)
- Pairs with: `joint.revolute`

### Prismatic joint — `joint.prismatic`

A slider between two planar bodies: relative motion along one direction only.

- Ports: a (planar_frame), b (planar_frame)
- Pairs with: `joint.revolute`

### Revolute joint — `joint.revolute`

A hinge between two planar bodies (a pin joint).

- Pairs with: `joint.prismatic`, `contact.wheel`

## Control

### Quantiser — `actuator.quantiser`

Rounds a signal to steps (a DAC or a coarse command).

- Ports: input (signal in Dimensionless), output (signal out Dimensionless)
- Pairs with: `control.pi`

### Constant signal — `control.constant`

A constant signal.

- Ports: value (signal out Dimensionless)
- Pairs with: `robot.h_bridge`, `part.brake`, `control.pi`

### Drive limiter (twist rate limit and deadman) — `control.drive_limiter`

Limits a requested body twist (speed and acceleration per axis) and stops it when requests stop arriving.

- Ports: request.forward (signal in LinearVelocity), request.lateral (signal in LinearVelocity), request.yaw (signal in AngularVelocity), sequence (signal in Dimensionless), twist.forward (signal out LinearVelocity), twist.lateral (signal out LinearVelocity), twist.yaw (signal out AngularVelocity)
- Pairs with: `control.command_lease`

### H-bridge gate driver (command −1…1 → four gates, sign-magnitude, synchronous) — `control.h_bridge_pwm`

An H-bridge gate driver: command −1…1 to four gate signals (sign-magnitude, synchronous).

- Ports: command (signal in Dimensionless), gate_hi_a (signal out Dimensionless), gate_hi_b (signal out Dimensionless), gate_lo_a (signal out Dimensionless), gate_lo_b (signal out Dimensionless)
- Pairs with: `electrical.mosfet`, `bridge.brushed_motor`

### Lag chain (transport delay) — `control.lag_chain`

A transport delay approximated by a chain of lags.

- Ports: input (signal in Dimensionless), output (signal out Dimensionless)
- Pairs with: `control.pi`

### PI regulator — `control.pi`

A continuous PI regulator on setpoint − measured (dimensionless signals).

- Ports: command (signal out Dimensionless), measured (signal in Dimensionless)
- Pairs with: `robot.h_bridge`, `electrical.voltage_sense`

### Finite pulse with scheduled command edges — `control.pulse`

A single pulse: on at `start` for `duration`, with exact edges.

- Ports: value (signal out Dimensionless)
- Pairs with: `robot.h_bridge`, `part.brake`

### PWM timer (duty 0…1 → gate) — `control.pwm`

A PWM timer: turns a duty cycle into a switching gate signal with exact edges.

- Ports: duty (signal in Dimensionless), gate (signal out Dimensionless)
- Pairs with: `electrical.mosfet`

### Sampled proportional controller — `control.sampled_p`

A proportional controller that updates only every sample period (like firmware).

- Ports: command (signal out Dimensionless), measured (signal in Dimensionless)
- Pairs with: `robot.h_bridge`

### Sinusoidal signal — `control.sine`

A sinusoid: for frequency sweeps and oscillating commands.

- Ports: value (signal out Dimensionless)
- Pairs with: `robot.h_bridge`, `actuator.pwm_driver`

### Angle setpoint (ramp + sine) — `part.angle_setpoint`

A commanded angle that ramps at a set speed and can wobble sinusoidally: the target for a stepper, servo or controller.

- Ports: angle (signal out Angle)
- Pairs with: `part.stepper_motor`, `part.pid_angle`, `robot.effective_servo`, `robot.servo_firmware`

### Complementary tilt filter — `part.complementary_tilt`

Estimates tilt from an IMU: trusts the gyro over short times and the accelerometer over long ones.

- Ports: accel_tilt (signal out Angle), ax (signal in LinearAcceleration), ay (signal in LinearAcceleration), gyro (signal in AngularVelocity), gyro_tilt (signal out Angle), tilt (signal out Angle)
- Pairs with: `part.tilt_imu`, `sensor.imu`, `part.pid_angle`

### Force command (ramp + step + sine) — `part.force_command`

A known force over time: a ramp that climbs steadily (then holds), a step held for a set time, and a sine. Feed it to a force source to push, pull or load a part.

- Ports: force (signal out Force)
- Pairs with: `translational.force_source`, `translational.mass`, `part.dowel_pin`, `part.dovetail`, `part.threaded_joint`

### Indexed moves (stop and shoot) — `part.index_move`

A commanded angle that steps by a fixed amount every period: a smooth move, then a still dwell for the photo.

- Ports: angle (signal out Angle)
- Pairs with: `control.sampled_fixed_pd`, `robot.servo_firmware`, `part.belt_drive`

### PI current controller (torque control) — `part.pi_current`

Holds a motor's current, and so its torque (τ = k·i), at a target by adjusting the drive command.

- Ports: command (signal out Dimensionless), measured (signal in Current)
- Pairs with: `sensor.current`, `robot.h_bridge`, `actuator.pwm_driver`, `bridge.brushed_motor`

### PID position controller (angle) — `part.pid_angle`

Turns an angle error into a motor drive command (−1…1): proportional, integral and filtered derivative action.

- Ports: command (signal out Dimensionless), measured (signal in Angle), target (signal in Angle)
- Pairs with: `robot.h_bridge`, `sensor.encoder`, `part.angle_setpoint`, `actuator.pwm_driver`

### PID position controller (linear) — `part.pid_position`

Turns a linear position error into a drive command (−1…1): proportional, integral and filtered derivative action.

- Ports: command (signal out Dimensionless), measured (signal in Length), target (signal in Length)
- Pairs with: `robot.h_bridge`, `sensor.linear_encoder`, `part.position_setpoint`

### Position setpoint (ramp + sine) — `part.position_setpoint`

A commanded linear position that ramps and can wobble sinusoidally: the target for a linear axis controller.

- Ports: position (signal out Length)
- Pairs with: `part.pid_position`, `translational.mass`

### Torque command (step + sine) — `part.torque_command`

A known torque over time: a step held for a set time, plus a sine that can sweep in frequency. Feed it to a torque source.

- Ports: torque (signal out Torque)
- Pairs with: `rotational.torque_source`, `rotational.inertia`, `rotational.spring`

### Trapezoidal move (limited acceleration) — `part.trapezoid_move`

A commanded angle that speeds up at a set acceleration, cruises, and slows down to stop exactly at the target.

- Ports: angle (signal out Angle)
- Pairs with: `part.stepper_motor`, `part.pid_angle`, `robot.effective_servo`, `robot.servo_firmware`

### Sampled servo position loop — `robot.servo_firmware`

A servo’s microcontroller loop: sampled PID with latency, dead band and sensor resolution.

- Ports: command (signal out Dimensionless), measured (signal in Angle), rate (signal in AngularVelocity), target (signal in Angle)
- Pairs with: `robot.h_bridge`, `robot.motor_unit`, `sensor.encoder`, `part.angle_setpoint`

- Pairs with: `thermal.capacitance`, `thermal.ambient`

### Controlled heat source — `thermal.controlled_heat_source`

Injects the heating power its input signal commands.

- Pairs with: `thermal.capacitance`, `thermal.temperature_sensor`

### Constant heat source — `thermal.heat_source`

Injects a constant heating power.

- Ports: node (thermal)
- Pairs with: `thermal.capacitance`
