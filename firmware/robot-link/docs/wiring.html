Every connection

#WireFromToCarries

Unverified until you check it on the bench

  • The P4 pins come from Waveshare's pinout for the ESP32-P4-WIFI6. Check that your -M board has 20 and 21 printed beside pins 6 and 7.
  • The top view of the dock here is the underside print mirrored left to right. Before power, use continuity to confirm the G7, G8 and GND sockets match their printed pads.
  • The robot link has passed its engine tests and a Verilator co-simulation against the real bridge.v and hx_safety.v. It has never run on this hardware.
  • The order of SIG, V+ and GND on the PH2.0 lead is only illustrative. Go by the servo's own label.

Rules that hold the whole way through

  • The FPGA is the only safety authority. The P4 only adds a transport.
  • Load FPGA images only with the motor supply off, and write a deployment receipt.
  • Drive the servos only with the operator at the bench and every servo supported.
  • Keep 11.1 V away from every 3V3, 5V and GPIO pin on both boards.
