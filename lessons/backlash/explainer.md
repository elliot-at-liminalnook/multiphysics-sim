## hook: A wobble you can feel
[[scroll top]] Grab the foot of a small walking robot, with its motors holding still, <short pause> and you can often wiggle it a few millimetres. That's backlash.

## gap: Room between the teeth
[[scroll block:room-between-the-teeth]] Gear teeth need a little room to mesh, and wear adds more. [[box figure:teeth "the gap"]] So the motor's tooth sits in a gap, with space on either side. [[unmark]]
[[highlight "for a moment it moves alone"]] Reverse the motor, and for a moment it moves alone, crossing the gap, while the output stays put. [[highlight off]] [[quiz reversal]]

## lost: How much is lost
[[scroll block:how-much-is-lost]] [[highlight "the output trails it by g"]] Pushing one way, the output trails the motor by half the play. [[highlight off]] So its swing comes up short, at both ends. [[quiz predict-swing]]
[[scene rock]] [[scroll scene:rock]] [[play-until 0.45]] Watch the reversal. <short pause> The motor turns back, and the pointer just waits.
[[play]] [[wait-scene]] On the phase plot, those flat edges are the lost motion. [[quiz flat-edge]]

## foot: At the tip of a leg
[[scroll block:at-the-tip-of-a-leg]] At a joint, play is an angle. <short pause> At the end of a leg, it becomes a distance: the angle times the length. [[quiz foot-slop]]

## fix: Living with it
[[scroll block:living-with-it-or-removing-it]] So what can a designer do? [[highlight "keep the teeth always pressed one way"]] Keep the teeth pressed one way, [[highlight "anti-backlash gears"]] use drives without play, [[highlight "put the position sensor on the output"]] or measure at the output. [[highlight off]] [[quiz fix]]
